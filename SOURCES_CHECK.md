# What went into the paper from the two research reports (checked 22 Nov 2026)

## Kept (checked against the original source)
- Chen et al. (2023), PNAS 120(51): GPT's budget choices are mostly rational and score higher than humans; robust to randomness and demographics; sensitive to framing. Only these qualitative findings are used, not the exact percentages.
- Brand, Israeli & Ngwe: HBS WP 23-062 (2023), later retitled "Using LLMs for Market Research" (rev. Apr 2026). LLM willingness-to-pay is often inaccurate and sometimes wrong-signed; use LLMs to supplement human studies, not replace them.
- Horton (2023): NBER w31122 now lists three authors (Horton, Filippas, Manning). refs.bib fixed.
- Hoq & Weninger (2026), arXiv 2604.15329: LLM surrogates are exploratory tools, not replacements.
- Banks, Blundell & Lewbel (1997), REStat 79(4): QUAIDS.
- Kumar, Kumar, Parappurathu & Raju (2011), AERR 24(1), 1-14: Indian food demand elasticities (QUAIDS and FCDS). Cereals have the lowest expenditure elasticities.
- Milk elasticities from the NDDB review: Kumar et al. 2011 expenditure elasticity 0.429 (FCDS) to 1.640 (QUAIDS); own-price -0.624 to -1.035. These are secondhand. Check them in the Kumar PDF tables before using the numbers.
- HCES 2023-24 (MoSPI press note): urban average MPCE Rs 6,996 (no imputation); bottom 5% urban Rs 2,376; top 5% urban Rs 20,310; food is about 40% of urban spending.
- Nandini milk: Rs 8/L hike from 1 Nov 2026; toned milk Rs 46 -> Rs 54; higher-fat variants rose more.
- Bengaluru auto fare: Rs 36 for the first 2 km, Rs 18/km after, from 1 Aug 2025.

## Dropped (could not verify, wrong, or not needed)
- Report 1's Table 1: lists Llama and Qwen3-32B models that Groq has retired.
- Report 1's citations to Ben-Zion 2025, Cherep 2025, Reusens 2026 and Prasad 2005: not verified, so not used.
- Report 1's price sources (CityCost, a supermarket blog): weak sources.
- Report 1's "placeholder" result numbers (e.g. staples elasticity -0.6): these are guesses and would bias the write-up.
- PDF's claim that a September 2026 study found no LLM setup reproduced more than 2 of 6 human effects: the arXiv paper it cites (2604.15329) is a different study.
- PDF's Cross et al. (2026) Veblen-effect results, Tak et al. and Aguiar & Kashaev (2026): not verified.
- PDF's exact Chen et al. percentages (95/89/81/92% and human CCEI 0.918): not confirmed, so not used.
- PDF's Tamil Nadu cereal elasticities (0.12 / -0.18): source not checked.
- PDF's free-tier limits from a practitioner blog and "Gemini 1,500 requests/day": these conflict with what the project measured. Use your own console numbers.
- PDF's suggestions to switch to conjoint designs, HCES-fractile budgets or paid Groq: these would change the locked design.
